一The doctor

Dr. Elena Marin, MD — dermatologist & cosmetologist

For twelve years Dr. Marin has practised a simple discipline: look closely, understand fully, and do only what the skin truly needs.

Trained in medical dermatology before specialising in aesthetics, she treats the full spectrum — from chronic acne, rosacea and eczema to the subtle work of softening lines and restoring radiance. Every plan is built on clinical evidence and a careful reading of your skin, your history and your intentions.

二Treatments

Two practices, one principle: care that is precise and unhurried.

Medical dermatology restores the health of the skin. Aesthetic cosmetology refines how it looks and feels. Most patients benefit from a thoughtful blend of both.

Medical Dermatology

医療

  • Acne & rosacea

    Prescription-led protocols that calm inflammation, clear congestion and prevent scarring.

  • Mole check & dermatoscopy

    Magnified, systematic examination and digital mapping for early, reassuring detection.

  • Eczema & psoriasis

    Long-term control with barrier repair, modern topicals and, where needed, systemic therapy.

  • Hair & scalp conditions

    Trichoscopy-guided diagnosis of hair loss and scalp disorders, with a clear way forward.

Aesthetic Cosmetology

美容

  • Botulinum toxin

    Micro-dosed precision that softens expression lines while keeping every expression yours.

  • Dermal fillers

    Hyaluronic acid to restore volume, refine contours and hydrate lips — always in proportion.

  • Chemical peels

    Gentle to medium-depth peels for pigmentation, texture, fine lines and lasting clarity.

  • Laser resurfacing & IPL

    Light-based renewal for sun damage, redness and scars, revealing an even, luminous tone.

  • PRP / mesotherapy

    Your own growth factors and tailored micro-infusions to revitalise skin and support hair.

  • Facials & skincare plans

    Medical-grade facials and a considered home ritual, so progress continues between visits.
