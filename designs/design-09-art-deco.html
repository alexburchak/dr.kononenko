Questions

Before your visit

No referral is needed. You can book a consultation directly online or by phone. If you have previous test results or a referral letter, please bring them along — they help us build a complete picture.

That is our guiding principle. Dr. Marin works conservatively, often in stages, to enhance your features rather than change them. A complimentary review two weeks after injectables allows fine adjustments.

Most injectables and light peels allow you to return to work the same day. Laser resurfacing and deeper peels may involve 3–7 days of redness or flaking. You will receive a clear timeline before any treatment.

For most adults, a full-body dermatoscopic check once a year is recommended. If you have many moles, fair skin or a family history of melanoma, we may suggest every six months with digital mapping.

We kindly ask for 24 hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel. Late cancellations may be charged 50% of the reserved treatment, so that we can offer the time to another patient.
