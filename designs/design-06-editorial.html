Profile — The Doctor
01 — AboutProfile · p. 04

The doctor will see you now.

Twelve years, eight thousand patients and one conviction: the best aesthetic result is healthy skin.

Dr. Elena Marin, MD

Dermatologist & Cosmetologist · Founder, Lumière

Dr. Elena Marin trained in medicine at the University of Copenhagen and completed her specialist residency in dermato-venereology, before a fellowship in aesthetic dermatology in Paris. For more than twelve years she has treated everything from chronic eczema and early skin cancers to the quiet signs of time.

Her practice rests on two principles. First, evidence: every protocol at Lumière is grounded in current clinical guidelines and published research. Second, restraint: she prefers small, precise interventions that let your skin, and your expressions, remain unmistakably yours.

Patients describe her consultations as unhurried and frank. She will tell you when a treatment is worth it, and, just as often, when it isn’t.

Nobody should be able to tell what you had done. They should only notice that you look well.— Dr. Elena Marin

02 — ServicesThe Index · p. 12

Two disciplines, one standard.

Medicine to heal the skin. Aesthetics to refine it. Every treatment begins with a diagnosis.

A.Medical Dermatology

Diagnose · Treat · Prevent

  • 2.1

    Acne & rosacea

    Prescription therapy, peels and light, combined to clear breakouts and calm redness without scarring.

  • 2.2

    Mole check & dermatoscopy

    Full-body skin cancer screening with digital dermatoscopy and mapped, comparable follow-ups.

  • 2.3

    Eczema & psoriasis

    Modern topicals, phototherapy and barrier repair for long, quiet stretches between flares.

  • 2.4

    Hair & scalp conditions

    Trichoscopy-based diagnosis of hair loss, scalp inflammation and dandruff, with a clear plan.

B.Aesthetic Cosmetology

Refine · Restore · Maintain

  • 2.5

    Botulinum toxin

    Micro-dosed to soften lines while keeping every expression intact.

  • 2.6

    Dermal fillers

    Hyaluronic acid to restore volume, hydrate lips and define contour, never overfilled.

  • 2.7

    Chemical peels

    From brightening fruit acids to medium-depth peels for pigment and texture.

  • 2.8

    Laser resurfacing & IPL

    Fractional laser and pulsed light for scars, redness, sun damage and uneven tone.

  • 2.9

    PRP / mesotherapy

    Your own growth factors, or vitamin micro-injections, to revive skin and support hair.

  • 2.10

    Facials & skincare plans

    Medical facials and a written home routine, adjusted with the seasons.

03 — Your journeyMethod · p. 18

In four chapters.

A clear sequence, from the first conversation to the last follow-up. No pressure, no guesswork.

Chapter one1

Consultation & skin analysis

A 45-minute appointment: your history, your goals and a dermatoscopic examination of your skin.

Chapter two2

Personal treatment plan

A written plan with options, timelines, expected outcomes and transparent costs. Take it home and decide.

Chapter three3

Treatment

Performed personally by Dr. Marin, using clinically studied, medical-grade products only.

Chapter four4

Aftercare & follow-up

A home routine, a check-in call and a review visit where results are measured and refined.
