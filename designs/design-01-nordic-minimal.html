01 About the doctor

Science first.
Then beauty.

For more than twelve years I have treated skin as what it is: a living organ that tells the story of your health. My work joins medical dermatology with aesthetic cosmetology, so the same careful eye that checks a mole also plans a subtle, natural refresh.

Education

MD, University of Copenhagen. Specialist training in dermatology and venereology, Rigshospitalet. Advanced aesthetic medicine fellowship, Paris.

Philosophy

Natural results, evidence-based medicine and honest advice, including when a treatment is not what you need.

Certifications

02 Services

Two disciplines,
one standard of care.

Medical concerns are diagnosed and treated with full medical rigour. Aesthetic treatments are planned with restraint, so results stay soft, balanced and unmistakably yours.

Medical Dermatology

Diagnosis and treatment for skin, hair and nails.

  • Acne & rosacea

    Targeted medical protocols that calm inflammation and prevent scarring, for teens and adults.

  • Mole check & dermatoscopy

    Full-body skin examination with digital dermatoscopy for early detection of skin cancer.

  • Eczema & psoriasis

    Long-term care plans that restore the skin barrier and reduce flare-ups.

  • Hair & scalp conditions

    Trichoscopy-based assessment of hair loss, dandruff and scalp sensitivity.

Aesthetic Cosmetology

Subtle, natural refinement, planned by a physician.

  • Botulinum toxin

    Softens expression lines while keeping your face fully expressive.

  • Dermal fillers

    Hyaluronic acid to restore lost volume and contour with a light hand.

  • Chemical peels

    From gentle glow peels to medium-depth renewal for texture and pigmentation.

  • Laser resurfacing & IPL

    Treats sun damage, redness and fine lines, and stimulates new collagen.

  • PRP / mesotherapy

    Your own growth factors, or tailored micro-nutrients, for skin and hair vitality.

  • Facials & skincare plans

    Professional treatments and a home routine prescribed for your skin type.
