Meet your doctor

Hello, I'm Elena — and your skin is in kind hands

I'm a board-certified dermatologist and cosmetologist with more than twelve years of clinical experience in Copenhagen and across Europe.

I trained in medicine at the University of Copenhagen, completed my dermatology residency at Bispebjerg Hospital and refined my aesthetic practice through fellowships in Paris and Milan. Today I treat everything from stubborn acne to subtle age-related changes — always starting with a proper medical diagnosis.

Treatments

Care for how your skin feels & looks

Healthy skin comes first. Beautiful skin follows naturally.

Acne & rosacea

Calm breakouts and redness with medical-grade protocols tailored to your skin type and triggers.

Mole check & dermatoscopy

Thorough head-to-toe screening with digital dermatoscopy for early, reassuring answers.

Eczema & psoriasis

Long-term plans that soothe flare-ups, restore the skin barrier and help you feel comfortable again.

Hair & scalp

Diagnosis and treatment of hair loss, thinning and scalp conditions using trichoscopy.

Botulinum toxin

Softens expression lines on the forehead, frown and eyes — subtle, rested, never frozen.

Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid to restore lost volume, define lips and refresh the mid-face with balance.

Chemical peels

Brighten tone, refine texture and fade pigmentation with peels matched to your skin.

Laser resurfacing & IPL

Targets sun damage, redness, scars and fine lines while stimulating fresh collagen.

PRP / mesotherapy

Your body's own growth factors and micro-nutrients to revitalise skin and support hair growth.

Facials & skincare plans

Professional facials and a simple at-home routine you'll actually enjoy following.

Most injectables and light peels have little or no downtime — perhaps mild redness for a few hours. Laser resurfacing may need 3–7 days of recovery. We'll plan your treatment around your calendar.
