The Doctor

Where medicine
becomes art.

“Beautiful skin is first of all healthy skin. I never treat a face I have not first understood.”

Dr. Elena Marin is a board-certified dermatologist and cosmetologist with more than twelve years of clinical experience. She works in person with every patient, offering what she missed in the industry: a practice where a medical diagnosis and a refined aesthetic eye work together, and where natural results are a matter of principle.

Education

MD, University of Copenhagen. Dermatology residency, Rigshospitalet. Aesthetic fellowship, Paris.

Philosophy

Evidence-based medicine. Natural results. Honest advice, always.

Credentials

Board-certified dermatologist. EADV member. Certified in dermatoscopy.

Member of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV)
