About the doctor

Dr. Elena Marin, MD

A board-certified dermatologist and cosmetologist, Dr. Marin has spent more than twelve years helping patients understand their skin — from complex medical conditions to the quiet confidence of a refreshed face. Trained in Copenhagen and Vienna, she brings Scandinavian precision and a warm, Mediterranean sense of care to every appointment.

“I don’t believe in changing faces. I believe in caring for skin so well that it tells your story beautifully.”

Treatments

Care for skin, inside & out

Two complementary disciplines under one roof: diagnosis and treatment of skin conditions, and refined aesthetic treatments for natural, lasting radiance.

I.

Medical Dermatology

Acne & Rosacea

Medical protocols to calm redness, clear breakouts and prevent scarring.

Mole Check

Digital dermatoscopy and body mapping for early, confident detection.

Eczema & Psoriasis

Gentle, long-term control with modern topicals, light therapy and biologics.

Hair & Scalp

Trichoscopy-led care for hair loss, thinning and scalp inflammation.

II.

Aesthetic Cosmetology

Botulinum Toxin

Softly relaxed expression lines — rested, never frozen.

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid to restore lost volume, contour and hydration.

Chemical Peels

Tailored acid peels for even tone, smoother texture and glow.

Laser & IPL

Resurfacing and light therapy for pigment, redness, scars and lines.

PRP / Mesotherapy

Your own growth factors and vitamin infusions for deep renewal.

Facials & Skincare Plans

Medical facials and a personal home routine, refreshed each season.
