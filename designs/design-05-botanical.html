About the doctor

Skin care that listens first, then treats.

For more than twelve years, Dr. Elena Marin has helped patients understand their skin, from stubborn acne and rosacea to subtle, graceful ageing. Trained in clinical dermatology and aesthetic medicine, she believes the best results are the ones nobody can quite name: you simply look rested, healthy and like yourself.

“Healthy skin is the foundation of beautiful skin. I treat the cause, respect the face’s natural architecture, and only recommend what the evidence supports.”Dr. Elena Marin · Treatment philosophy

Treatments

Medicine for the skin, gently applied.

Two disciplines under one roof: medical dermatology to diagnose and heal, aesthetic cosmetology to refine and restore. Every plan begins with understanding your skin.

Medical Dermatology

Diagnose & heal

Acne & rosacea

Calming, scar-preventing protocols combining prescription therapy, peels and light treatments.

Mole check & dermatoscopy

Full-body skin cancer screening with digital dermatoscopy and mapped follow-up.

Eczema & psoriasis

Long-term control of flares with modern topicals, phototherapy and barrier repair.

Hair & scalp

Trichoscopy-led diagnosis of hair loss, dandruff and scalp inflammation, with clear treatment paths.

Aesthetic Cosmetology

Refine & restore

Botulinum toxin

Softens expression lines while keeping movement natural. Micro-dosed, never frozen.

Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid to restore lost volume, hydrate lips and refine contours with restraint.

Chemical peels

Fruit-acid to medium-depth peels for glow, pigmentation and fine texture.

Laser resurfacing & IPL

Fractional laser and intense pulsed light for scars, redness, sun damage and tone.

PRP & mesotherapy

Your own growth factors and vitamin cocktails to revitalise skin and support hair growth.

Facials & skincare plans

Medical facials and a home routine built around your skin type, season and goals.
