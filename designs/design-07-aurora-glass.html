Meet your doctor

Precision medicine, gentle hands.

Dr. Elena Marin is a board-certified dermatologist and cosmetologist with more than twelve years of clinical practice across Copenhagen and Vienna. She founded Lumière to bring medical rigour and aesthetic sensitivity under one roof.

Her philosophy is simple: treat the skin as an organ first, then enhance what is already there. Every plan is evidence-based, carefully dosed and designed for results so natural that people notice you look rested — not “done”.

Treatments

Two disciplines. One skin strategy.

From diagnosing a suspicious mole to softening expression lines, every treatment is planned by the same doctor who knows your skin.

Most booked

Acne & rosacea treatment

Targeted medical protocols — topical and oral therapies, peels and light-based treatment — to calm inflammation, clear breakouts and prevent scarring.

Mole check & dermatoscopy

Digital mapping and magnified analysis for early detection.

Eczema & psoriasis care

Long-term control with modern topicals, phototherapy and biologics.

Hair & scalp conditions

Trichoscopy-guided care for hair loss, alopecia, dandruff and scalp inflammation — with PRP and medical therapies when indicated.

Signature

Botulinum toxin

Micro-dosed relaxation of expression lines on the forehead, frown and crow’s feet — fresh, never frozen.

Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid to restore volume, contour and hydration.

Chemical peels

Glycolic, salicylic and TCA peels for tone, texture and glow.

Laser resurfacing & IPL

Fractional laser and intense pulsed light for pigmentation, redness, scars and fine lines.

PRP / mesotherapy

Your own growth factors and vitamin cocktails for renewal.

Professional facials & skincare plans

Medical facials, hydrafacial-style deep cleansing and a home routine built on clinically proven actives — reviewed every season.
