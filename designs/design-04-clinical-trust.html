01 — About the doctor

Clinical precision, with a human approach.

For more than 12 years I have treated patients with one principle: an accurate diagnosis comes before any treatment — medical or aesthetic.

I'm a board-certified dermatologist and a member of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV). My practice spans complex inflammatory skin disease, skin cancer screening and minimally invasive aesthetic medicine. I only use treatments supported by peer-reviewed evidence and CE-approved products, and I explain the expected benefit, risk and recovery of every option in plain language.
